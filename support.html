Support

IQ Puzzle · Last updated: July 31, 2026

Need help? Email brightminds.support@gmail.com

We usually reply within two business days. To help us answer faster, please mention your device model, your iOS version, and the app version (Me › Settings, at the bottom of the screen).

Getting started

IQ Puzzle needs no account and no sign-up. Open the app and start playing. Four games — One Line, Ball Sort, Hidden Cats and Color Blocks — are free, along with all 200 One Line levels and the well-being check-ins.

What IQ Puzzle Pro unlocks

Pro unlocks the seven remaining games (Block Blast, Tower of Hanoi, Connect Dots, Color Flow, Arrow Line, Math Audit and Connect Balls) and lets your daily plan draw on all eleven.

  • Weekly — $0.29 for the first 7 days, then $19.99 per week.
  • Yearly — $39.99 per year.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us, so cancelling happens in iOS:

  • Open Settings and tap your name at the top.
  • Tap Subscriptions, then IQ Puzzle Pro.
  • Tap Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, otherwise the next period is charged. You keep access until the period you already paid for runs out.

I paid but Pro isn't active

Open the Pro screen (the PRO badge in the top-right of any tab) and tap Restore in the top-right corner. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you purchased with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us and include the date of purchase.

Refunds

All purchases go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple as well. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. We can't issue refunds ourselves, but we're happy to help you sort out what went wrong.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every game runs entirely on your device. A connection is only needed to start, restore or renew a subscription.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress, streaks and check-in answers are stored on your device only, which means they are private — and also that deleting the app deletes them. There is currently no cloud sync.

About the check-ins

The quizzes in the Tests tab (life satisfaction and personality traits) are for personal reflection only. They are not a medical or psychological diagnosis, and they are not an IQ test. If something in your life is troubling you, please talk to a qualified professional.

Privacy and your data

We collect a small amount of anonymous app and device data and never ask who you are. The details are in our Privacy Policy. To request deletion of data associated with your installation, email us from the address you'd like us to reply to and mention "data deletion".

Report a bug or suggest a game

Both very welcome — same address: brightminds.support@gmail.com. Bug reports that describe what you did just before the problem appeared are the ones we can fix fastest.
